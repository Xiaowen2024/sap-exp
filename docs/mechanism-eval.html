1. How to read this library

Each card separates literal-default B=1 from frozen B=5. The public view foregrounds a task success rate when that task has a preregistered pass criterion, then only the one or two metrics that expose the contact mechanism. Full candidate sweeps stay in the linked artifacts. B=5 measures bounded engineering usability; it is not a universal simulator ranking or a substitute for real-world trajectory validation.

Publication rule: complete positive and complete negative conclusions are shown below. Runs with incomplete force/contact audit, incomplete held-out transfer, or no comparable SAP model are explicitly listed as pending rather than silently scored.

2. Static friction

This category tests whether an engine predicts the expected stick-versus-slip transition under sub-Coulomb and super-Coulomb loads. The primary reported metric is the fraction of repeated rollouts whose retain/eject label matches the preregistered expectation.

2.1 Three-box push

Three 1-kg boxes are vertically stacked. A horizontal load pushes either the middle box or the top box. The middle interface has an analytic breakaway load of 9.81 N; the top interface has 4.905 N. The 14 primary force conditions use R=20 perturbations each.

4. Other completed conclusions

These entries have final artifacts. A task without a preregistered binary success gate reports its frozen held-out loss or fidelity metric rather than an invented success percentage.

SAP retains contact duty 1.0 without truncation on its held-out tests, but the strict force/settling gate fails at the reported 0.25- and 1-ms dt variants; this limitation remains visible rather than being averaged away.

5. Pending audit or not comparable

  • GAUGE slope slider, MIT Push, cube-toss sustained tail: numerical B=1/B=5 runs exist, but force/contact-health telemetry still needs independent audit before a mechanism conclusion is published.
  • Pendulum and Newton cradle: B=1/B=5 selection is complete; frozen held-out amplitude/dt transfer remains pending.
  • Cassie landing: no comparable SAP result: the current importer does not preserve the Menagerie ball-joint/generalized-coordinate semantics.
  • Peg-in-hole and DENSE EZGripper: no compliant B=1/B=5 artifact to publish yet.
