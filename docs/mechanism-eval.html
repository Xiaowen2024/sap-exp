1. How to read this library

Each card reports a single task only after SAP Warp and MuJoCo have completed the comparable artifact. B=5 is the primary engineering-usability measure: it asks whether a literal default plus at most four declared native candidates can recover the preregistered task outcome. It is not a universal simulator ranking or a substitute for real-world trajectory validation.

Current scope: only the first static-friction card is published below. Other completed or incomplete tasks are intentionally not summarized here until they are reviewed and added individually.

2. Static friction

This category tests whether an engine predicts the expected stick-versus-slip transition under sub-Coulomb and super-Coulomb loads. The primary reported metric is the fraction of repeated rollouts whose retain/eject label matches the preregistered expectation.

2.1 Three-box push

Three 1-kg boxes are vertically stacked. A horizontal load pushes either the middle box or the top box. The middle interface has an analytic breakaway load of 9.81 N; the top interface has 4.905 N. The 14 primary force conditions use R=20 perturbations each.
