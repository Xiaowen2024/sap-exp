Mechanism Aware
Experiment Protocol

A compact, task-first protocol for evaluating contact-rich simulation. It separates physical fidelity, internal simulation health, and the usability of a frozen native configuration.

Families
Five atomic families + composite tasks
Selection rule
Literal B=1 and declared B=5
Robustness rule
Freeze first; evaluate held-out second
Protocol principle. A task may report any metric that has the required evidence. Missing physical data invalidate only the corresponding physical-fidelity claim—not otherwise healthy simulation-level or frozen-setting evidence.

1. How to read every family

Each family begins with the same three shared layers. Its task table then adds only the metrics that answer that task's distinct physical question. “Real-world fidelity” is reported only with a synchronized physical reference; otherwise the result is explicitly simulation-level.

Shared layerQuestionMinimum reporting rule
1. Physical fidelityDoes simulation match a physical trajectory, event, force, or outcome?Use registered frames and a declared window. Report only metrics supported by released or measured reference data.
2. Simulation consistencyIs the simulated mechanism internally healthy?Check the task-relevant contact, force, geometry, impulse, equilibrium, or conservation quantity; report capacity truncation and invalid states.
3. Frozen-setting usabilityCan an engine's native setup be selected and transferred responsibly?Compare literal default B=1 with up to four declared native candidates (B=5), choose only on calibration data, freeze the winner, then report repeats and held-out coverage.
Repeated trials. For discrete outcomes, state the positive class, numerator/denominator, and confusion matrix. For continuous measures, report the unit, window, and variation across declared samples or local perturbations. Do not turn a continuous task into an arbitrary success percentage.

2. Static friction stick / retain

Use when tangential load remains below the static friction limit and the object should stay fixed relative to its support or gripper.

Shared protocol

LayerShared metricsInterpretation / gate
Physical fidelitySupport-frame trajectory RMSE, DTW, relative displacement and tangential-velocity errorUse only with synchronized physical motion; DTW accompanies, never replaces, time-aligned RMSE.
Simulation consistencyTail relative displacement, tail creep, friction utilization ρ, contact duty, normal-load error/CV, gap fluctuationSticking needs persistent support; a reasonable mean normal force alone does not rule out intermittent impulse support.
Frozen-setting usabilityPass@B, first passing budget, physical-range sweep, local dt/pose/seed robustness, setup transferFreeze native contact settings before varying mass, friction, load ratio, or geometry.

Task-specific additions

TaskAdditional metricsTask question
Three-box pushExpected retain/eject agreement; TP/FP/FN/TN, FPR/FNR, balanced accuracy, confidence intervalsDoes the selected contact model predict the static breakaway boundary of a stacked contact interface?
Two-finger simple pinchMinimum measured per-finger hold force; grip-force error; final lift-height errorDoes the gripper establish enough normal force to retain and lift the object without slip?

3. Dynamic friction sustained sliding

Use when the intended mechanism is sustained kinetic sliding in a declared support frame.

Shared protocol

LayerShared metricsInterpretation / gate
Physical fidelityTrajectory RMSE, DTW, fitted tangential acceleration error, terminal displacement errorFit acceleration over a predeclared sliding window; do not differentiate noisy mocap samples frame by frame.
Simulation consistencyAnalytic acceleration and terminal-displacement error, ρ, contact duty, normal-gap/height errorFor continuous Coulomb sliding, ρ should be near one and support must remain present.
Frozen-setting usabilityPass@B, frozen mass/friction/slope/speed ranges, local robustness, setup transferExpected binary outcomes and thresholds are task-specific and preregistered before repeats.

Task-specific additions

TaskAdditional metricsTask question
Slope SliderNo extra scalar: it uses the full shared trajectory, acceleration, support and friction diagnosticsDoes the model reproduce gravity-driven sliding and the stick-to-slip transition against physical trajectories?
MIT PushFinal planar pose error; stable-window wrench error and twist errorDoes a recorded pusher motion yield the correct object pose and coupled force–motion response?

4. Dynamic impact impulse / rebound

Use for short normal-contact transitions where pre- and post-impact state, restitution, and impulse validity matter.

Shared protocol

LayerShared metricsInterpretation / gate
Physical fidelitySupport-frame trajectory RMSE and DTWUse a registered physical event window; pair RMSE with DTW rather than using phase-tolerant distance alone.
Simulation consistencyEffective restitution, contact duration, impulse closure where a unique resultant wrench is definedCheck post-impact energy behavior and momentum consistency; do not sum ambiguous multi-contact rows as a physical wrench.
Frozen-setting usabilityPass@B, mass/velocity/drop-height/geometry transfer, R≥20 local draws, setup transferDefault, mechanism-guided B=5, and any disclosed historical brute-force ceiling are separate stages.

Task-specific additions

TaskAdditional metricsTask question
Bouncing ballPer-bounce apex-height error and normalized apex-decay errorDoes normal impact reproduce the observed sequence of energy loss?
Cube tossFinal position/orientation/resting-face error; post-bounce linear and angular speed-decay errorDoes the simulator reproduce a coupled edge/corner impact outcome?
Cassie landing (impact profile)Landing success; foot re-liftoff/chatter; residual torso oscillation; weighted 50-ms state errorDoes a touchdown transient remain stable under the declared robot and controller convention?

5. Energy conservation long-horizon dynamics

Use for conservative or near-conservative systems where numerical drift, dissipation, and momentum transfer are the core mechanism.

Shared protocol

LayerShared metricsInterpretation / gate
Physical fidelityTrajectory RMSE and DTW, when synchronized physical motion is availableOtherwise compare against the declared analytical Hamiltonian or conservation law.
Simulation consistencyRelative mechanical-energy drift/loss against the theoretical profileConservative systems target zero drift; passive systems must not exhibit unphysical energy growth.
Frozen-setting usabilityPass@B, amplitude/initial-energy/scale transfer, energy-drift versus dt, setup transferState integrator, substeps, damping/friction, and solver tolerances explicitly.

Task-specific additions

TaskAdditional metricsTask question
PendulumPeriod duration from dominant-frequency analysis; period error or ratioDoes the integrator retain the nonlinear oscillation timing over a long horizon?
Newton's cradleMomentum transfer efficiency; longest stationary duration of intermediate ballsDoes impulse transfer reach the outgoing ball without artificial intermediate-ball jitter?

6. Sustained contact settled equilibrium

Use after transients have ended and the contact system should remain settled, supported, and geometrically valid.

Shared protocol

LayerShared metricsInterpretation / gate
Physical fidelitySteady position/height error; aggregated static normal-force errorRequires synchronized resting-state geometry and, for force fidelity, calibrated load-cell/force-plate data.
Simulation consistencySigned-gap/equilibrium overlap, normal-load balance error, contact duty, normal-force CVEvaluate only after a declared settled window begins. A run that never settles is a task failure.
Frozen-setting usabilityPass@B, stack/load-ratio sweep, R=20 local and dt robustness, support-geometry transferSave the contact witness/gap convention and exclude all impact frames from this family.

Task-specific additions

TaskAdditional metricsTask question
Cube toss (settled tail)Planar drift rate and yaw-drift rate over the steady windowDoes the final resting object creep or rotate under nominally static gravity support?
Three-plate stackStack-order preservation; gap-aware center-of-mass height bias; thresholded stack success; thickness breakdown sweepCan a thin multi-contact stack retain order, load closure, and geometric validity?

7. Composite tasks coupled geometry, actuation & contact

Composite tasks intentionally have no universal scalar score. Each retains the same three layers, but selects task-specific diagnostics for coupled geometry, control, contact switching, and mechanisms.

Shared protocol

LayerShared metricsInterpretation / gate
Physical fidelityUse registered task-specific pose/state/outcome errors only where a physical reference existsDo not infer real-world fidelity from a simulation-only success rate.
Simulation consistencyTask-defined geometry, penetration, force-closure, constraint, or contact-switching diagnosticsThese are health gates and mechanism explanations, not a universal cross-task ranking.
Frozen-setting usabilityLiteral B=1 versus fixed B=5; physical-control ranges; R≥20 local robustness; held-out setup transferFreeze material, collision representation, and controller conventions before held-out evaluation.

Task-specific additions

TaskAdditional metricsTask question
Round peg-in-holeSuccess rate; peak penetration; penetration/clearance ratio; final contact mode (inserted, unseated, jammed, escaped)Can the coupled contact geometry complete insertion without penetration-assisted success?
DENSE EZGripper graspSlip/no-slip prediction and balanced accuracy; object-pose drift; friction margin; wrench residual; penetration; contact duty/switchingDoes the underactuated grasp predict retention and the accompanying object motion?
Cassie landing (composite profile)Impact/recovery weighted state errors; loop-closure residual; normalized foot penetration; tail internal-shear ratioDoes articulated, closed-chain, multi-foot contact remain physically valid through impact and recovery?
GLIDE-inspired tomato plate transferPlate lift, tilt, tomato spill count, contact-capacity health, finite-state checksCan a declared GPU surrogate carry a loaded plate without loss of support or excessive tilt?

8. Records required for every run

RecordWhat to saveWhy
Scene and inputsGeometry/asset identity, mass/inertia, friction/material convention, gravity, controls, initial state, timestep, duration, reference frameMakes the physical task and collision representation recoverable.
Selection and evaluation splitLiteral B=1, all B=5 candidates, calibration-only data, frozen setting, held-out cases, repeat/seed distributionPrevents selection evidence from being presented as transfer evidence.
Metric validityWindows, contact aggregation/witness rule, force or impulse closure where needed, capacity-truncation flags, missing telemetryMakes each reported metric auditable at its own scope.
